Information Collection and Use

The Service collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Applications and Games that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent using the Service
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • In games with an online mode: the nickname you choose for online games (chosen freely by you — please do not use your real name), a random anonymous player identifier, your connection status and the moves you play during an online game
  • In games that offer notifications, and only if you allow them: a notification registration token for your installation (a device identifier, not linked to your name or e-mail address)

The Service does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device, and never asks you for location permission. Advertising networks may derive an approximate location — at the level of a city or a region, deduced from your IP address — in order to choose which advertisements to show; see Advertising, and Apple devices below.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using any of the Applications and Games, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

Online Game Modes and Leaderboards

Some of our games offer an online multiplayer mode and leaderboards. When you use these features, the following services process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — creates an anonymous player identifier (a random user ID) so that you can join online tables. No name, e-mail address or password is requested.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google, servers located in the European Union) — stores the nickname you choose, your anonymous identifier, your connection status and the moves played during an online game, so that the game can be shared with the other players at your table. Your nickname is visible to the other players of the table. Online game records are temporary and are deleted automatically after the game (normally within a few hours); anonymous accounts that have not been used for 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Player reports — when you report another player (offensive nickname, insults, cheating), the report (the reported player's anonymous identifier and nickname, your anonymous identifier, the reason and the time) is stored so that we can review it and, if needed, ban the account from online play.
  • Player ID and leaderboards — the game draws a player ID for you on first launch, in the form Name_Nickname_key (for example Amine_Tayara_gY764km88). It is generated on your device, contains no personal information, and is never linked to a Google account. Nothing is sent to our servers until you win your first game: at that point your Name_Nickname and your number of wins are published to the in-game leaderboard, where other players can see them. The key (the part after the last underscore) is never shown to other players and never appears in the leaderboard — it is what lets you restore your ranking on another phone, so keep it to yourself.

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Notifications

Some of our games — currently Ronda 3D — can send you notifications about the game itself: that a player is waiting alone in the online lobby, or that someone has opened a table with friends and shared its code, so that you can join that table in one tap.

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) assigns your installation a registration token. It is a device identifier, it is not linked to your name or e-mail address, and it is used only to deliver these notifications.
  • Your device subscribes to two topics: a general one, and one derived from your anonymous player identifier. The second is never used to send you anything — it exists so that our server can avoid sending you your own notification.
  • When you look for an online match, or when you press “3lem nass” on a table you opened, the nickname you chose is included in the notification sent to the other players, together with the five-character code of the table when you share one. Nothing else about you is sent, and nicknames pass an automatic filter before being broadcast.
  • These notifications are sent by our own server code, are rate-limited, and are never used for advertising. No third party can use this token to send you anything.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the game (Settings → Notifications), or in your phone's system settings. Turning them off unsubscribes your device, and no further message is sent to it.

Advertising, and Apple devices (iPhone and iPad)

Our games are free and are funded by advertising, served by Google AdMob. What is done with your data for those advertisements depends entirely on the answers you give when the game first starts.

  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any advertisement is loaded. You choose there whether your data may be used for personalised advertising. You can reopen that form at any time from the game's settings and change your answer.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iPhone and iPad only). On Apple devices, iOS additionally asks you, in a system dialog, whether you allow the app to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you answer Ask App Not to Track, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not read, and the advertisements you see are not personalised. If you allow it, that identifier may be used to personalise and measure advertisements. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • SKAdNetwork (iPhone and iPad only). If you install one of our games after seeing an advertisement for it, Apple may send the advertising network a report saying that the installation happened. That report is produced by Apple itself: it is aggregated, delayed, and contains no identifier that would allow anyone to recognise you.
  • Approximate location. Advertising networks derive an approximate location — at the level of a city or a region, deduced from your IP address — in order to choose which advertisements to show and in which language. The games never read your device's precise location and never ask you for location permission.
  • Measurement. Google Analytics for Firebase sends measurement data to app-measurement.com. On Apple devices this address is declared as a tracking domain, which means it is contacted for tracking purposes only when you have allowed tracking.
  • Refusing changes nothing to the game. Whether you decline the European consent form, refuse tracking, or both, every feature stays available — including online play, private tables, leaderboards and notifications. Only the advertisements become non-personalised.

Our games are distributed on Google Play and on the Apple App Store. Apple and Google each apply their own privacy policy to the store itself; the present policy covers only what happens inside the games.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the specific Application or Game easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

You may refuse notifications when your phone asks, or switch them off later in the game (Settings → Notifications) or in your phone's settings, without losing any other feature.

On iPhone and iPad, you may withdraw tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In Europe, you may reopen the advertising consent form from the game's settings and change your answer. In both cases the game remains fully playable; only the advertisements become non-personalised.

You may stop using the online features at any time. To delete your leaderboard data yourself, open the game, go to Leaderboards → My account → “Delete my data”: your leaderboard row and your player ID are erased from our servers immediately. You can also have any data linked to your anonymous identifier deleted by contacting us at zmi.amn@gmail.com.

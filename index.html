🔒 Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications and games published by Yamano (hereby referred to as "Service Provider" or "Yamano"), collectively referred to as the "Applications and Games". This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Service collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Applications and Games that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent using the Service
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • In games with an online mode: the nickname you choose for online games (chosen freely by you — please do not use your real name), a random anonymous player identifier, your connection status and the moves you play during an online game

The Service does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using any of the Applications and Games, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid the Service Provider in improving the Applications and Games and their service. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.

Please note that the Applications and Games utilize third-party services that have their own Privacy Policy about handling data. The following is a list of services that may be used by our Applications and Games (though not all third parties are present in every app):

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database (online game modes)
  • Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

Online Game Modes and Leaderboards

Some of our games offer an online multiplayer mode and leaderboards. When you use these features, the following services process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — creates an anonymous player identifier (a random user ID) so that you can join online tables. No name, e-mail address or password is requested.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google, servers located in the European Union) — stores the nickname you choose, your anonymous identifier, your connection status and the moves played during an online game, so that the game can be shared with the other players at your table. Your nickname is visible to the other players of the table. Online game records are temporary and are deleted automatically after the game (normally within a few hours); anonymous accounts that have not been used for 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Player reports — when you report another player (offensive nickname, insults, cheating), the report (the reported player's anonymous identifier and nickname, your anonymous identifier, the reason and the time) is stored so that we can review it and, if needed, ban the account from online play.
  • Google Play Games Services — if you choose to sign in with your Google Play Games profile, your scores (number of wins) are submitted to the game's leaderboards and shown to other players together with your Play Games profile name and avatar, according to your Play Games privacy settings. Signing in is optional; the game works without it.

  • with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.
